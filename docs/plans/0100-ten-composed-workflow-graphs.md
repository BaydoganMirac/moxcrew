## Problem

Task0099 proves explicit frozen handoffs for J5, while the 1.0 baseline requires
ten composed Project workflows. Six older bundled recipes still flatten their
members into generic phases, three compositions have no bundled entry, and the
desktop therefore cannot present the remaining required chains as inspectable,
reusable work.

## Scope

1. Upgrade launch, podcast, study, travel, content and research recipes to new
   revisions that represent J1, J2, J3, J4, J7 and J9 with explicit earlier-
   member dependencies. Keep old frozen recipe bodies replayable.
2. Add bounded career-package, event-package and meeting-follow-up recipes for
   J6, J8 and J10. Preserve the legacy data recipe and the verified J5
   data-briefing recipe unchanged.
3. Use no more than six members per composition. Every final reviewer depends
   on every producer, while intermediate tasks receive only the exact checked
   upstream files declared by the graph plus frozen owner inputs.
4. Keep prepare/start, provider assignment, local-tool readiness, budgets,
   external-app preview/dispatch and owner decision acceptance as separate
   existing authority boundaries.
5. Localize every new or changed built-in name, description, visible member,
   deliverable and tool requirement in Turkish without translating owner/model-
   supplied recipe content.
